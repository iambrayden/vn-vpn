Questions

What does "devices at a time" mean?

You can set up {{ brand }} on as many phones, tablets and computers as you like. Your plan decides how many of them can be connected at the same moment — Basic allows {{ plans.basic.devices }}, Pro allows {{ plans.pro.devices }}. Switching is instant: disconnect one, connect another.

Which apps do I need?

Just the free, official WireGuard app for your device. After you subscribe you'll get a QR code or file for each location — import it and flip the switch.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes, anytime from your account. Upgrades take effect immediately (you only pay the difference); cancellations stop the next renewal.

How do you handle my data?

Your connection to our servers is always encrypted. See our privacy policy for exactly what we record and how long we keep it.
